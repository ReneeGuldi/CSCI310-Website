About Me

I am a Computer Science student at the University of Indianapolis, passionate about software development and web technologies. I earned my Associate of Applied Science in Software Development from Ivy Tech Community College in May of 2025. My journey in programming has led me to explore various aspects of software engineering, from front-end development to back-end systems, full-stack development, database management, automation, and building tools that improve workflow efficiency and usability. I enjoy creating interactive and user-friendly applications that solve real-world problems and enhance the user experience. In my free time, I like to work on my own personal projects and stay up to date on the latest trends and technologies in the software development industry.

Before software, I spent four-plus years on the other side of the screen: verifying DOT compliance files and documenting patient care in EHR systems. Being the person who actually uses workflow software every day has taught me to start with a user's pain point, to test a single change at a time, and pick the metric that tells the whole story.

Alongside my academic experiences, I also have several years of professional experience working with:
